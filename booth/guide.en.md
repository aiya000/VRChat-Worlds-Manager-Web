# Getting started with VRChat Worlds Manager Web

A web app for organising your VRChat favorite worlds into folders.
It runs in your browser, so there is no installer to download.

<p class="url"><a href="https://vrcww.com">vrcww.com</a></p>

## Getting started

1. Open **https://vrcww.com** in your browser
2. Add it to your home screen or desktop (optional)
3. Finish the first-time setup and sign in with your VRChat account

## Detailed steps

The steps for each device, what happens to your sign-in details, how to use it in VR, and where to ask for help are all on the page below.
That page is kept up to date, so it is always newer than this PDF.
